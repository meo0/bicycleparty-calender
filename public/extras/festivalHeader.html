Bike Summer June 1–August 31, 2025

Bike Summer (aka Pedalpalooza) is a festival of bikey fun. Hundreds of different events are organized by people like you. Most rides are free and all are open to the public to join.

Bike Summer is three whole months of fun on two wheels. View the archives of past festivals. If you need help creating or editing an event, check out the calendar FAQ or contact the calendar crew.

Check Bike-Summer.org for updates, follow Bike Summer on Instagram, and tag posts about rides @pedalpaloozapdx!
